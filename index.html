Illustrative examples. Newton writes the command for your own system.

Three keys. That's the whole idea.

You never leave the prompt, and you always see the command before it runs.

Say it

Type what you want the way you'd say it, like kill whatever is using port 3000.

Press Tab

Your sentence is replaced by the command. Press Tab again to cycle other ways to do it. Shift+Tab brings your words back.

Review, then Enter

Newton never runs anything for you. Risky commands get a plain warning before you decide.

More than one trick.

Tab is the heart of it. Around it are the tools you reach for when a task is bigger or something goes wrong.

Tab to command

Plain English becomes a command in your prompt, in zsh, bash or fish. Normal completion still works for everything else.

Plan mode

Describe a bigger job and get a checklist. Approve it one step at a time, with undo, pause and resume.

Ask and chat

Pipe an error into newton ask or open a chat, and get answers in the terminal without opening a browser.

Fix

When a command fails, newton fix reads what happened, explains it, and suggests the repair.

Commit messages

Stage your changes and run newton commit. It writes the message from the diff and your recent style.

Safety built in

Dangerous commands are flagged, and secrets are redacted before anything leaves your machine.

Big jobs, one step at a time.

Ask for a whole setup and Newton lays out a checklist. Nothing happens until you say so, and you stay in charge the whole way.

  • Approve, skip or edit every step
  • See what each step does and how long it took
  • Undo, pause and pick it up again later
  • Export the plan as a script or Markdown

Install in a minute.

You need Python 3.9 or newer and git. The installer adds Newton to your PATH and turns on Tab for your shell.

# get Newton
$ git clone https://github.com/asteya01/newton
$ cd newton
# install and run the guided setup
$ sh install.sh

Then open a new terminal and try find every file over 100MB in my home folder, then press Tab.

Run newton doctor if anything looks off. It checks your setup and tells you what to fix.
